工会称 Rockstar 开发者工会规模或超 1600 人，涵盖几乎全部英国基层员工

原文标题：Rockstar developer union could end up over 1,600 workers strong and encompass nearly all UK staff below senior management, IWGB claim

导读

此前报道过，由英国 Rockstar Games 开发者组成的工会，其承认投票定于 GTA 6 主机版 11 月 19 日发售前后举行。与 Rockstar 员工合作组建工会的英国独立工人工会（IWGB）如今透露，一旦投票通过，工会覆盖的人数将相当可观，可能超过 1600 人，几乎涵盖英国所有高级管理层以下的员工。

原文摘要

As we've previously covered, a vote on recognition of a union made up of UK-based Rockstar Games devs is set to take place around GTA 6 's console launch on November 19th. The Independent Workers of Great Britain (IWGB) union, whom the Rockstar staff are working with in their efforts to organise, have now revealed just how many workers the Rockstar could end up encompassing if the recognition vote is successful. It's not a small number.
