《County of Fortune》现已开启抢先体验：一款中世纪城市建造游戏——为什么只建一座城？

原文标题：County of Fortune, now out in early access, is a medieval city builder that asks, why limit yourself to just one city?

导读

我知道你想要什么。区区一个城市建造游戏已经不够了，对吧？你需要的是多城建造，不能只停在一座城，但又不想在完全不同的存档里另起一座城——最好都在同一张地图上，开车一程就到。如果是现代城市，我帮不了你；但如果是横跨整个郡的多座中世纪城镇，《County of Fortune》正合适。

原文摘要

I know what you want. The humble city builder isn't enough for you any more, is it? You need a cities builder, you can't just stop at one, but you don't want to build up an entirely different city on a new save file. If it were up to you, they'd all be on the same map, a motorway's worth of a drive away from each other. If we're talking contemporary cities, I can't help you, but if you can settle for numerous medieval towns across an entire county, then County of Fortune should do just fine.
