“教训就是，两者可以兼得”：《Skyrim》首席设计师认为，《Baldur's Gate 3》式的艰难抉择与 Bethesda 的自由玩法各有价值

原文标题："The lesson learned is you can have both": Skyrim lead designer reckons there's merit in Baldur's Gate 3-style tough choices and Bethesda's do-anything approach

导读

RPG 更好的是优先保证玩家自由，还是每前进一步就关掉几扇门、逼你换个角色再打一遍？《Skyrim》首席设计师认为，两种做法只要执行到位各有价值，这场争论恐怕到 4000 年还会继续。

原文摘要

Whether RPGs are better off prioritising player freedom or endeavouring to have several doors slam shut every time you put another foot forwards in order to encourage multiple runs with different characters is, to put it bluntly, something I reckon will still be a matter of debate in the year 4000. We'll all have evolved in non-corporeal beings floating in a galaxy that's been racked by such change that even the six million copies of The Elder Scrolls 5 we've littered the Earth with will have been swept away into the great recycling bin on the gods' desktop, and there'll still be sniping going on about whether it's ok that Skyrim lets you be a master thiefssassin who leads the companions, knows every spell under the sun, and slapped up a world-eating dragon for fun. For what it's worth, the game's lead designer reckons both games that do and don't let you do that have their merits when executed well.
