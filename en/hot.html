How is heat calculated?

Each item is first scored by rules: base 50 points; freshness (+25 within 6 hours, +20 within 24 hours, +10 within 72 hours); Official sources +10; general gaming media -15; each developer keyword hit (tutorial, tool, data, open source, guide, postmortem, engine, optimization, performance, revenue, sales, experience, source code, workflow) +5, capped at +25; each noise keyword -5, floor -25; total capped at 100. Heat = score × 0.8^days (decaying daily), looking back 7 days only. Trends compare with the previous list: up 2 or more places counts as rising, down 2 or more as cooling, a 1-place move as steady; first-time entries are marked “New”.
